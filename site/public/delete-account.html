How to delete your Finova account

Finova is an app by Arthur Rios. You can delete your account and your data in two ways.

In the app

  1. Open Finova and sign in.
  2. Tap My Account, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you cannot open the app, email arthur.rios007@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Delete Finova account”. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email and user ID) is deleted permanently and at once.
  • When you delete from the app, all data stored on the device is deleted too: transactions, budgets, cards, allocations, tags and profile photo.

What is kept

Nothing. We keep no copies after deletion.

See also our Privacy Policy.
